Paul Morrow free Baybayin TrueType fonts (Windows releases from paulmorrow.ca):
  BayLopez.ttf  — Baybayin Lopez (1620)
  BikMintz.ttf  — Bikol Mintz (1835)
  BisHerv.ttf   — Bisaya Hervás (1787)

Tagalog Doctrina 1593 and Tagalog Stylized are already under src/assets/fonts/baybayin/.
Each face maps Baybayin glyphs to both Latin keys and Unicode Tagalog.
